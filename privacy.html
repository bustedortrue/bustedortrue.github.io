Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy covers Busted or True Publisher (the "Tool"), a private application used only by the owner of the Busted or True channel to publish videos to the channel's own accounts.

YouTube API Services

The Tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using the Tool, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the Tool accesses

  • The OAuth authorization of the channel owner's own Google account, used to upload videos, thumbnails, captions and video metadata (title, description, tags, translations, schedule).
  • Statistics and reports of the owner's own channel, read through the YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs.

The Tool does not access, collect or store data from any other channel, viewer or user.

How data is used, stored and shared

  • Authorization tokens and channel statistics are stored only on the owner's computer, outside any public location.
  • Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to evaluate their performance.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Stored tokens and statistics are deleted from the owner's computer when access is revoked, or on request by email.
